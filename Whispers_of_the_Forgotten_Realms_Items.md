# Region: Verdant Woods

## Weathered Compass

**Internal Name:** ITEM01

**Description:** A small brass compass scratched and dulled by years of travel. Its needle trembles constantly but eventually settles toward the north.

**Initial Location:** VW01

## Silver Acorn

**Internal Name:** ITEM02

**Description:** A tiny acorn cast from tarnished silver. Delicate lines etched into its cap resemble the branches of an ancient tree.

**Initial Location:** VW05

## Bundle of Raven Feathers

**Internal Name:** ITEM03

**Description:** Three glossy black feathers bound together with faded red thread. Their surfaces flash blue and purple when turned toward the light.

**Initial Location:** VW10

## Crystal Water Vial

**Internal Name:** ITEM04

**Description:** A stoppered glass vial filled with perfectly clear spring water. Tiny points of green light drift slowly through the liquid.

**Initial Location:** VW11

## Carved Wooden Fox

**Internal Name:** ITEM05

**Description:** A palm-sized wooden fox polished smooth by frequent handling. One ear has been chipped away and its eyes are marked with tiny spots of black paint.

**Initial Location:** VW08

## Runestone Fragment

**Internal Name:** ITEM06

**Description:** A broken piece of grey stone bearing part of a deeply carved rune. The fragment feels unexpectedly warm despite the cool forest air.

**Initial Location:** VW20
